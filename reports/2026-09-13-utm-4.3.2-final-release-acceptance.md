## Root cause and correction

The remaining 4.3.1 portable defect was in the local PowerShell invocation, not in the published R2 bytes or update feed. The helper script read its archive and destination from `$args`, while the prior child-process invocation did not deliver those values in the required form. The resulting null `LiteralPath` caused `Expand-Archive` to fail before replacement.

The 4.3.2 correction keeps the change narrow:

- invokes `powershell.exe` directly with `-NoProfile`, `-NonInteractive`, and `-ExecutionPolicy Bypass`;
- embeds the archive and destination paths using PowerShell single-quote escaping, including apostrophes, spaces, ampersands, and Unicode;
- does not use a `cmd` shell or unsafe command interpolation;
- preserves the existing download hash, size, archive inspection, version-marker, replacement, rollback, and cleanup checks;
- keeps the explicit destination and hidden helper process behavior.
